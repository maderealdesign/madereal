Questions

Before you send the menu.

Do I need a new website?

No. Your menu can have its own clean link and can be linked from your current website, Google profile, social pages or a QR code.

Why is there a monthly cost when your websites are one-off?

A standard MadeReal website remains a one-off purchase. This menu is a separate managed system with a live editor, database, hosting, software updates, maintenance and ongoing support.

Can more than one member of staff use it?

Yes. We can create access for the people responsible for keeping the menu up to date.

Can you copy the menu we already have?

Yes. Send a website link, PDF or clear photographs. We structure the dishes, prices and dietary information, then design the customer menu around your brand.

Will every restaurant menu look the same?

No. The editor and data structure stay reliable, but the customer-facing design is created for each restaurant.
