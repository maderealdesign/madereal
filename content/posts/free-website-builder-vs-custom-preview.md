# Free Website Builder vs a Free Custom Website Preview

A free website builder and a free custom website preview solve different problems.

A builder gives you software. A custom preview shows you what a designer would create for your business.

Both can be sensible choices. The right one depends on your time, confidence and what the website needs to achieve.

## Choose a free website builder when:

- you enjoy learning new software;
- you have time to write and arrange every page;
- the website is a temporary test;
- you only need a very simple online presence;
- you are comfortable checking mobile layouts, forms and search settings yourself.

Website builders can be useful for getting an idea online quickly. The main cost is often your own time.

Check the paid features before starting. Your own domain, removal of adverts, additional pages, forms and search tools may require an upgrade.

## Choose a custom website preview when:

- customers will judge the quality of your business from the site;
- you need calls or enquiries, not merely an online page;
- you are unsure how to structure the content;
- you want your services and local area explained properly;
- you would rather review a real design than build everything yourself.

A good preview uses your business name, services, photographs, reviews and contact routes. It gives you something specific to assess without asking you to become a web designer first.

## Time and effort

The software behind a builder may be easy to use, but the important decisions still belong to you:

- What should the headline say?
- Which services need their own sections?
- What will make customers trust the business?
- Where should the enquiry button appear?
- Which towns should the site mention?
- How will the page work on a phone?

A custom preview puts those decisions into the hands of somebody who builds business websites regularly. You still provide corrections and final approval, but you do not begin with a blank screen.

## Design and credibility

Templates are not automatically bad. Many professional websites begin with a reusable structure.

The difference is how carefully that structure is adapted. A local electrician needs different proof and enquiry routes from a restaurant, consultant or shop.

## Search visibility

Neither a builder nor a designer can guarantee Google rankings.

Useful search foundations include:

- a clear page title and main heading;
- accurate service and location information;
- mobile-friendly pages;
- sensible internal links;
- fast loading;
- a Google Business Profile;
- genuinely useful content.

A builder may provide the controls, but you still need to decide how to use them. A custom business website should include those foundations as part of the structure.
