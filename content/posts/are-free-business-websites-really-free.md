# Are Free Business Websites Really Free?

Some are free to start. Very few complete business websites are free forever.

The word “free” can describe several different offers:

- a do-it-yourself website builder with restrictions;
- a free trial that becomes a subscription;
- a basic website included with another paid service;
- a free design mock-up;
- a free website preview before paying for the finished build.

The important question is not simply “Is it free?” It is “Which part is free, what happens next and who owns the finished website?”

## Common costs behind free website offers

A free website builder may charge when you want to:

- use your own domain name;
- remove the builder’s advertising;
- add contact forms or booking tools;
- access useful search settings;
- connect analytics;
- add more pages;
- move the site somewhere else.

None of those charges is automatically unreasonable. The problem is when the limitations are not clear until you have already spent days building the site.

Before accepting any offer, check the monthly price after the trial, the contract length, what happens if you cancel and whether you can export the website.

## What should a clear offer tell you?

A trustworthy website offer should answer these questions in plain language:

1. What is free?
2. What does the finished website cost?
3. Are there compulsory monthly fees?
4. Who owns the website?
5. Is hosting included or separate?
6. What happens if you do not continue?
7. What support is included after launch?

If those answers are vague, ask before handing over card details or moving your domain.
