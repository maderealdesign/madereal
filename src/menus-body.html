A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Questions about
online menus?

Will guests need to download an app?+

No. They open your menu link or scan a QR code and browse in their phone’s browser.

Can we keep our existing website?+

Yes. Your menu can have its own link, which you can add to your website, socials and printed QR codes. You can see a venue preview at Trawden Arms.

What do the dietary filters mean?+

They display the dietary tags your venue confirms for its dishes, including options available on request. Dietary tags do not guarantee that a dish is safe for an allergy. Guests should talk to your team about ingredients, substitutions and preparation before ordering.

Can our staff add dishes and change prices?+

That is the core workflow: an authorised team member edits the menu in a simple workspace. Try the interactive example above to change prices, photos, categories and availability.

What about paper menus?+

The demo produces a clean A4 menu from the current available dishes, grouped by category. Use your browser’s print dialog to print it or save it as a PDF.

Does MadeReal manage bookings or take food orders?+

This pilot focuses on displaying and updating your menu. Keep using your existing booking and ordering systems; their links can sit alongside your menu.

How do we get started?+

Send your venue details below. We’ll show you the menu experience and agree your setup and pilot terms before you join. We’ll confirm your venue setup and pilot terms before opening your staff account.
